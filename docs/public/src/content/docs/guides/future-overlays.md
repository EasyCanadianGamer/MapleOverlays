title: Future Overlays
description: MapleOverlays has two overlays today, Chat and Now Playing. More overlay types are planned.
---

MapleOverlays has two kinds of overlay today:

- [Chat overlay](/guides/chat-overlay/): your Twitch chat, styled your way.
- [Now Playing](/guides/nowplaying-overlay/): the track playing on your Last.fm account.

More overlay types are planned, but they are not decided yet. This page will list them once they are.
